Photography
===========

Replace every placeholder marked "placeholder" in the site with authentic
Cistern of Hope Foundation photography.

Use imagery showing:
  children learning, young people participating, women building livelihoods,
  community interaction, mentorship, education, environmental and community
  activity, people collaborating.

Do NOT use:
  extreme poverty imagery, images designed to provoke guilt, generic stock
  photos of "Africa", unrelated international imagery, or any image that
  compromises a child's dignity.

Before publishing any photograph of an identifiable person:
  1. Confirm written, informed consent is held (for a child, from the parent
     or guardian, and the child's own assent where appropriate).
  2. Do not caption an image with information that identifies a child's
     school, home or exact location.
  3. Write alternative text that describes the image respectfully.

Recommended upload sizes:
  Hero            2400 x 1350 px
  Card / feature  1400 x 1050 px
  Portrait         900 x 1125 px
  Logo (partner)   600 px wide, transparent PNG or SVG
